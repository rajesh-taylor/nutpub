Judge for yourself

Three gigs, three ways artists get paid with Cashu ecash over HTTP 402.

Have your phone ready for the doormen and the bar staff (or press Let in by hand and Pour by hand) and end every gig on Takings, moving your sats to Cashu.me.

Peggy Sue’s Music Bar

Venue

Online

Buy the online ticket, say hello in the chat, then tip with a comment and watch the stretch goal move.

Watch online is one 402 (NUT-24); each tip is another, or a Nutzap signed with your Nostr key (NIP-61). Every payment is ecash locked to Longy's key (NUT-11): the server checks it, and can't spend it.

60 sats a minute

Watch online, pay as you watch: play, stop, come back, tip. You pay only for the minutes you watch.

Each 10-second piece is its own 402: 10 sats (NUT-24), locked to Frankie's key (NUT-11). Stop the video and nothing more is charged.

3 · At the venue

Frankie Peroni

Second night · The Fickle Pickle

Venue
£30, first drink free
Online
Free, tips welcome

Ticketing with the venue: buy the venue ticket, get let in at the door, then get your first drink at the bar. At home, watch free and tip.

The ticket's door QR changes every 30 seconds (TOTP), so a screenshot won't get in. The drink is locked to the bar's key until last orders, then falls back to Frankie (NUT-11 locktime); the bar spends it at the mint and the fan's phone sees it go (NUT-07).
