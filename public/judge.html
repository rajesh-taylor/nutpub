Last orders for the middlemen

For fans who’d rather keep their bank card in their pocket, and artists who’d rather not keep a spreadsheet of their fans: a whole night out in Cashu, from the ticket and the door to the first round, the stream, the tips and the replay.

3 · The night at the venueLaptop + phone

Longy

The Fickle Pickle

Venue
£35, first drink in it, scanned at the door
Online
Free, tips welcome

Set the price and turn on the door. Your phone becomes the doormen’s: it scans the fan’s ticket, a QR that changes every 30 seconds. Then the first drink, a coin on a QR, poured by the bar. No phone? Let in by hand, Pour by hand.

The ticket is a 402 (NUT-24), locked to Longy’s key (NUT-11); its door QR is a 30-second code (TOTP), so a screenshot won’t get in. The drink is the coin itself: ecash locked to the bar’s key, as a loop of QR codes (NUT-16, proposed); not poured by last orders, it goes back to Longy. The free stream is paid up front by Longy: a 402 locked to the NutPub’s key.
