A night out using Cashu. From the ticket, door entry, first round, 402 streaming payments, comments & tips and takings back to your own wallet.

Each gig builds on the last: run them in order.

3 · The night at the venueLaptop + phone

Longy

The Fickle Pickle

Venue
£35, ticket scanned at the door, free drink included
Online
Free, tips welcome

Set the price and turn on the door: your phone becomes the doormen’s scanner for the fan’s ticket. Save the ticket as a passkey, if you like. Then the bar pours the free pint, a coin on a QR. No phone? Let in by hand, Pour by hand.

The ticket is a 402 (NUT-24), locked to Longy’s key (NUT-11), and blind-signed by the gig (NUT-00, with a NUT-12 proof): the door knows it’s real and used once, never who bought it. Its QR is signed again every 30 seconds, so a screenshot won’t get in. The drink is the coin itself: ecash locked to the bar’s key, as a loop of QR codes (NUT-16, proposed); not poured by last orders, it goes back to Longy.
