Tips and Nutzaps

Tip button

One tap, for every fan. Any other amount goes in the comment box.

Nutzap buttons

One-tap tips under the chat, for fans signed in with Nostr: public, with their name. Up to three. Leave an amount empty for fewer.

Live stream

Stream from your phone with Larix Broadcaster (iPhone or Android): install, scan this QR with your phone, then press the red button in Larix. Free Larix puts its name over your video; Larix Premium $9.99/mo removes it.

On the phone itself? Open in Larix

In Larix, under Settings → Connections, only NutPub should be ticked: Larix streams to every ticked connection.

For the full wide picture, set Orientation to Landscape in Larix's settings before you start. Most people who pay will watch on a laptop. Turning the phone during the stream only leaves black bars.

Streaming from a laptop: put these in OBS (Settings → Stream → Custom).

Keep the key and the code private: anyone with them can stream as you.

Producers

Someone you trust to run your nights: Plan gig, Show Time, your stream and Pub TV. Never your 12 words, your takings, splits or invites. Up to two.

Moderators

For people you trust, in the room or at home. They see your stream before it opens, chat, post links (guests can't), delete messages and use slow mode. The main mod can change the pin. Never your pages, your takings or your stream key. Up to three.

Words to keep out

On top of the NutPub’s own list of slurs. One per line, or commas between. A message with one of them isn’t sent.

Your Nostr mute list

People and words you’ve muted on Nostr stay out of your chat too.

Public entries only. Show Time looks for a newer list each time it opens. ·
