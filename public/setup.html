Live stream

Stream from your phone with Larix Broadcaster (iPhone or Android): install, scan this QR with your phone, then press the red button in Larix. Free Larix puts its name over your video; Larix Premium $9.99/mo removes it.

On the phone itself? Open in Larix

In Larix, under Settings → Connections, only NutPub should be ticked: Larix streams to every ticked connection.

For the full wide picture, set Orientation to Landscape in Larix's settings before you start. Most people who pay will watch on a laptop. Turning the phone during the stream only leaves black bars.

Streaming from a laptop: put these in OBS (Settings → Stream → Custom).

Keep the key and the code private: anyone with them can stream as you.
