### Live wiring (session 3, Sun 1 Nov): plan
Longy streams from Larix; a viewer at home pays as they watch. Account ID and API token are Worker secrets (`STREAM_ACCOUNT`, `STREAM_TOKEN`, Stream: Edit), never in the repo.
1. **Live input per artist** (`server/live.js`, built): `POST /api/admin/live` makes `nutpub-<page>` once (recording automatic, `deleteRecordingAfterDays: 30`, signed URLs off until step 4), keeps its id in kv `live:<page>`, and fills the setup page's server, key and playback link.
2. **Setup page, plain** (built, tested Sun 1 Nov: QR → Larix → picture in the dashboard after ~20 s; the dashboard's "Disconnected" badge doesn't update by itself, so trust the picture): a "Make my stream" button; the Larix QR (`larix://set/v1?conn[][url]=<server+key>&enc[vid][res]=1280x720&enc[vid][keyframe]=4&enc[record][enabled]=off`), fetched with the admin key (it holds the stream key); server + key with Copy for OBS.
3. **Gig page** (built, tested Sun 1 Nov on the Mac: live light, then the phone's picture with sound after ~20 s; free to watch until step 4; DVR not tried, no seek bar yet): hls.js (from npm, bundled, so iPhone Safari 17.1+ works through ManagedMediaSource) plays the input's HLS; DVR to test. **Live light** from the input's `status` (`connected`/`reconnected` = on), read by the Worker at most every 10 s and passed in the 2-s poll.
4. **The gate (built and tested Mon 2 Nov, iPhone with Larix → Safari on the Mac):** a Stream signing key made once (secrets `STREAM_KEY_ID`, `STREAM_KEY_JWK`), self-signed RS256 tokens (no API call, no rate limit), signed URLs on. `GET /api/live/token`: a ticket gets a token; otherwise the usual `cashuGate` per 10 s, and the paid reply carries a token with `exp` = now + 30 s (slack for a slow payment). hls.js swaps the newest token into every request, so the picture runs on while payments keep coming. The viewer sets a budget ("up to 500 sats tonight") and Pause; no payment, the token runs out and the picture stops. 
   **Test results:** the plain link gets 401. `sub` can be the input id or the live video's id: we use the input id, which never changes. Once `exp` passes, the manifest, the playlists and even a segment already listed all get 401, so the picture stops. Only playlist links need the fresh token; segment links carry their own signature. Don't change a live input's settings during a stream: playback dropped out for a moment (204, no live video listed).
   **Also built:** one row per viewer (`live_paid`) for tonight's spend, so a lost reply asked for again within 8 s gets no second charge. Viewer checks passed: pay to watch, budget, Pause, rejoin, ticket watches free, wallet empty, stream end.
   **Larix:** wide needs Orientation = Landscape in Larix's settings (the setup page says so). Not yet tried on the Pixel: Larix isn't installed there. Paid live video needs iOS 17.1+.
   **Not closed:** a tampered page could pay every 30 s instead of every 10, and a token could be shared for its 30 s (accepted for a test concept). Collect now works in tens with a progress count; test it past 150 payments.
   **Real mints' fees, from their own /v1/keysets, 2 Nov (input_fee_ppk: 100 = 0.1 sat per coin):** Minibits 0, Coinos 100, Voltz 100, Cuba Bitcoin 100, Macadamia 150. All five support NUT-07 and NUT-11. The site stays on the test mint; Rajesh is emailing Minibits, Coinos and Macadamia about a small test for the docs' figures.
   **Mint fee (decided 2 Nov: charge per minute):** the test mint charges 0.1 sat per coin, at least 1 sat per swap. A viewer pays about 11 sats for 10, and Collect swaps each payment alone, so the artist gets about 9 of the 10. Fix: collect all payments in one swap, say "plus a small mint fee", and count the fee in the budget (500 today can cost about 550).
   **Built 3 Nov (session 5):** the show's price is now `stream.perMinute` (saved shows: old price × 6, so 10 became 60). Live: a token lasts 80 s, a payment every 60 s; the server tells the page how long is left of a paid minute, and asking again inside it costs nothing, so a rejoin is free. The recorded set stays at 10-s pieces, at a sixth of the rate. Collect: up to 500 coins a swap; a failed swap is halved until a bad coin stands alone (the gate doesn't check DLEQ, so a forged coin could otherwise block the lot); Takings says what the mint kept. The fee is per coin, so one swap doesn't remove it: it removes the rounding up. The viewer's fees count towards the limit (kept in the browser).
- **Free plan, per viewer paying every 10 s:** 720 Worker requests an hour (402 + paid retry) beside **5,400** for the 2-s poll (it asks for the show, the stage and the chat: 3 requests every 2 s); 100,000 a day ≈ **16 viewer-hours**. Folding the three into one `/api/tick` brings it to ~2,500 an hour, ≈ 40 viewer-hours: do it before the judges come. Over the limit the site stops answering until midnight UTC; it never costs money. CPU: one RS256 signature (~1 ms) and the token checks; the NUT-07 call is waiting, not CPU. Segments come from Stream, not the Worker. **So: charge every 10 s.** If judges push the daily limit, 30 s is a one-number change.

### Artist sign-in and the key's backup (agreed 2 Nov; before anyone uses a real mint for a real gig)
Today the artist's secret key lives only in the browser that opened Takings: no backup. Clearing site data, a private window, another device or Safari's 7 days lose it, and uncollected sats stay locked for good. Plan: each artist signs in on Set up your gig with a passkey (this is part 2's invite + passkey). A padlock on the page shows it's protected. The key is sealed in the browser with a secret only the passkey can produce (WebAuthn PRF), and our server keeps only the sealed copy, which it can't open, so "never custody" holds. Signing in on another device opens it. A recovery code covers a lost passkey or a browser without PRF. Also: "Make a new key here" must say it won't recover old payments, and the show's close reminds the artist to collect.
Agreed with Rajesh, 2 Nov: artists are enrolled one by one; no gig can be created without a Lightning address (Longy has a Lightning wallet); at setup the page says "write this down" and shows the recovery code, then makes the passkey. The passkey lives in iCloud Keychain on an iPhone, Google Password Manager on Android, or a password manager such as free Proton Pass for anyone without those. Check Proton Pass supports PRF, not only passkeys, and research GrapheneOS.
The recovery code: 12 words, as Proton and Tuta do. Enrolment test: Rajesh sends each new artist 21 sats, to prove their Lightning address works. Takings must say the smallest amount worth sending out, from the mint's fee reserve and the receiving wallet's own limits (ask the mints; Rajesh recalls Fountain having a 1,000-sat minimum in summer 2026, unchecked).
Test first: does PRF work on the iPhone (Safari) and the Pixel (Vanadium on GrapheneOS)? Sealing follows the encryption rule: WebCrypto AES-GCM, a key from HKDF with `refueler.nutpub.artist-key.v1`, a fresh random nonce each time, known-answer tests. Check whether Coco's key can be restored from a code, or change how it's made.

**The plan (session 5, 3 Nov; no code yet).**
1. **The 2-minute test, on each phone:** open Corbado's demo, https://webauthn-passkeys-prf-demo.explore.corbado.com/ , register a passkey, sign in, and see whether both steps give the same PRF value. iPhone Safari (needs iOS 18.4 or later); Pixel in Vanadium with Proton Pass; Pixel with anything else on hand. Write down yes or no for each. It's their site, not ours, so if the iPhone and Pixel say yes, repeat once on thenutpub.com before building (a small test page in the sign-in session).
2. **What the research says (secondary sources, 3 Nov; the test decides):**
   - iPhone: iCloud Keychain gives PRF, iOS 18.4+ for the data-loss bugs to be fixed (Corbado, updated Sept 2026; dev.to). Likely yes.
   - Android with Google Password Manager: yes by default. Needs Google Play services, so normally not on a plain GrapheneOS phone.
   - **Proton Pass: sources disagree.** Corbado tested yes on macOS with Chrome; a mid-2026 dev.to table says not yet; a Reddit user says no on iOS. No word from Proton, and no phone result. Don't promise it.
   - **GrapheneOS/Vanadium: no source on PRF at all.** Forum reports on passkeys there conflict. AliasVault's docs (Oct 2025) said Android's Credential Manager blocked PRF for third-party apps, but Corbado's newer data has KeePassDX working on Android 16 with Chrome, so that may have changed. Try KeePassDX if Proton Pass fails.
   - Bitwarden is weak (0% on iOS Safari, 29% on Android in Corbado's data); Dashlane and NordPass: no; 1Password: yes but refuses Vanadium, by forum report.
3. **So the recovery code is the real backup, and the passkey the convenience.** Every artist gets the 12 words whatever the phone says. No PRF: the page says "no padlock on this browser: keep your 12 words", and they type them on a new device.
4. **Coco's key:** the key is already derived from the wallet's seed (`m/129373'/10'/0'/0'/i`), so the seed restores it. But today's seed is 64 random bytes in localStorage, which no 12 words can make. Change: make 12 words first (128 bits, BIP-39), and the seed from them; the seed gets sealed, not just the key. New artists only: Longy's current key gets a clean break, after a final Collect. `coco.wallet.restore()` also brings back coins already collected.
5. **Sealing:** PRF output (with a fixed salt) → HKDF-SHA256, `info = utf8("refueler.nutpub.artist-key.v1") ‖ 0x00` → AES-GCM over the seed, a fresh 96-bit nonce every time. A second sealed copy under a key from the 12 words, `refueler.nutpub.artist-recovery.v1` (never the same key for both jobs). Known-answer tests for both. The server stores only the two sealed copies, the credential id and the public key. It never sees the words or the PRF value.
6. **Order of build:** test → invite + passkey sign-in (replaces the admin key) → 12 words + Lightning address at enrolment → sealed copies → new device → recovery → padlock → "Make a new key here" warning → remind to collect at the close → smallest amount worth sending out. About 2 or 3 sessions; before anyone uses a real mint for a real gig, so after the demo if time runs short (the demo stays on the test mint).
Sources: [Corbado](https://www.corbado.com/blog/passkeys-prf-webauthn), [dev.to](https://dev.to/ddyy/beyond-login-encrypting-data-with-passkeys-and-webauthn-prf-p20), [AliasVault](https://docs.aliasvault.net/mobile-apps/android/autofill.html), [Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/subreddits/ProtonPass/snapshots/2026-03-07T02%3A05%3A43.687412Z), [GrapheneOS forum](https://discuss.grapheneos.org/d/12019-passkeys-as-mfa-on-grapheneos-a-guide).
